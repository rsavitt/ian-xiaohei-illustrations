# Style DNA

## In one sentence

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, lots of white space, restrained, weird, product-sketch feel, hand-written feel, structure-clear-but-not-a-manual.

Like a sketch a long-time AI/product/design/dev-tools person would scribble onto a sheet of white paper to explain something.

## Must

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage paper feel.
- Mostly black hand-drawn line art: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector-perfect, not heavy outlines.
- Lots of white space: the main subject takes ~40%–60% of the canvas, with at least 35% blank, ideally one whole quiet area.
- A few handwritten English labels: at most 5–8 labels, each ideally 1–4 words.
- One image explains only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Structure should be expressed naturally — never write the structure type name on the image.

## Colors

- Black: main line art, character, frames, structure, primary text, main objects.
- Red: key callouts, problems, emotional beats, critical warnings, results.
- Orange: main process, paths, arrows, automation flow, A-to-B movement.
- Blue: supplementary notes, mental states, system states, second-layer explanations, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required in every image. Be restrained — when in doubt, use less, not more.

## Absolutely not

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographics.
- No formal flowcharts.
- No course slides.
- No cute cartoon posters.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagrams.
- No polished flat illustration.
- No tech-y UI.
- No real app screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, textures.
- No exhaustive labeling of every node.
- No top-left titles like "Workflow / System Architecture / Common Pits / Roadmap."

## Aesthetic direction

Aim for weird, creative, interesting, clean, freewheeling.

Not cute, not childish, not over-complex, not stiff.
